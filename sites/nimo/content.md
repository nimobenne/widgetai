### [Build-Readiness Dashboard for InvenTree](https://github.com/nimobenne/inventree-build-readiness)

Build-readiness dashboard on top of InvenTree: how many kits can ship today, and which part is the bottleneck on the rest. Read-only by default.

Language: Python · Updated: September 2026

How many can we ship today, and what is stopping the rest? InvenTree knows every part and every stock level, but answering that one question still meant opening several BOMs and doing arithmetic by hand. This is a small Flask app that answers it on one screen. For each kit assembly it shows how many are buildable from same-day stock, how many more become buildable once a one-day transfer lands, and which single part is the bottleneck on the rest.

### [Satellite Cybersecurity Testbed (Flatsat) — Solo Systems Build](https://github.com/nimobenne/portfolio/tree/main/satellite-cybersecurity-testbed)

Type: First solo engineering assignment, in progress, July 2026

Stack: NASA cFS (Core Flight System), Proxmox/Scale Computing HCI, Suricata, Zeek, Wazuh SIEM — all open source

Updated: August 2026

Real satellites can't be used for cybersecurity testing or training — they're single-instance, must stay operational, and touching a flight vehicle is not an option. A "flatsat" solves this by replicating satellite subsystems on the ground so researchers can attack, defend, and train against something realistic without risk. Existing flatsats are bespoke, fixed-lab installations, expensive to replicate. The assignment: build one that runs on a single portable edge-compute node, entirely from open-source software, as a general capability demonstration — a working proof, not a slideware pitch.
